Problem:
Display a chart (bar chart) based on data in Table 1: Total Sales of Product A.

1. IPO Model:
--------------------------------------------------
Input   : Monthly sales data (Months and Amounts)
Process : 1. Iterate through each month and sales amount.
          2. Compute number of asterisks (e.g. amount / 100).
          3. Print month label followed by horizontal bar of asterisks and the numeric amount.
Output  : Graphical bar chart representing sales data

2. Pseudocode:
--------------------------------------------------
START
    DECLARE array months = ["January 2016", "February 2016", "March 2016", "April 2016", "May 2016", "Jun 2016"]
    DECLARE array amounts = [2500, 1600, 2000, 2700, 3200, 800]
    
    PRINT "=== BAR CHART: TOTAL SALES OF PRODUCT A ==="
    
    FOR i = 0 TO 5 DO
        PRINT months[i] + " | "
        SET barLength = amounts[i] / 100
        FOR b = 1 TO barLength DO
            PRINT "*" (without newline)
        ENDFOR
        PRINT " (" + amounts[i] + ")"
    ENDFOR
END
